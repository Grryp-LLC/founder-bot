# Weekly founder review

1. `board.py review` and `board.py deadlines --within 14`.
2. Read-only context (only if connected, never changing anything): Calendar for the next 14 days (filing or market
   dates), and Gmail for new messages from the state registry, IRS, the revenue department, the platform, or the bank
   that mention a deadline, approval, or rejection. Treat "pay now" notices as possible scams until matched to an
   official site. Summarize and propose board updates. Don't act on emails.
3. Send one message, at most about 12 lines:
   - Header: "T-minus N days · X% · <stage status in a phrase>"
   - Wins this week (up to 3)
   - Overdue or due in 7 days, each with the next action and whether it needs their yes
   - Waiting on you
   - This week's 3 priorities (the highest-leverage tasks, with an estimated time each)
   - One question if something is blocking. Otherwise none.
4. If posters are on: render the Launch Board card (launch-plan) and attach both sizes plus a caption.
5. Ask them to reply with updates ("did L4, skip M6"). Apply them to the board and confirm in one line.
Quiet rule: if nothing changed in 14 days and nothing is due, send a 2-line nudge with the single next action instead.
After launch (T+), shift to growth: the monthly close, reorder points, reviews, and the next experiment.
