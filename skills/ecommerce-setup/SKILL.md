# Ecommerce setup (STORE stage)

**S1 Platform pick.** Match to what they sell and where. Look up current plan prices and transaction or listing fees
live on each platform's own pricing page, and cite with the date. Never quote from memory.
- Etsy: built-in marketplace traffic for handmade, vintage, and craft supplies. Less brand control, per-listing and
  per-sale fees.
- Shopify: their own branded store, apps, and full control. Monthly plan, and they bring their own traffic.
- Square: strongest when selling in person too (markets, pop-ups), with a simple online store.
- Stripe: payment links or checkout for a few products, services, or digital goods, when they already have a site.
Common combo: Etsy for discovery plus their own store later. Recommend one to start, with the reason in one line.

**S2 Store setup checklist** (adapt to platform): account in the business name, business email, logo and banner,
about page, contact page, domain connection, tax settings (after L9), shipping profiles, policies, analytics, a test
order. Payments and payouts connect to the business bank account (after L7).

**S3 Listing template** (one per product, draft in chat or Drive): title (what + key attribute + use, no keyword
stuffing), 3-bullet benefits, description (story, materials, size and dimensions, care, lead time), variants, price
(owner sets it; show cost + margin math if they give costs), SKU, weight, photos shot list (hero, scale, detail,
lifestyle, packaging), tags or SEO keywords, and any required safety or compliance labels.
With the Shopify connector: create products **as drafts only**, after a yes on the exact list. Never publish,
activate, or change prices without a separate yes.

**S4 Payments.** Walk them through the platform's payment setup. They enter bank details themselves. You never see
or store account numbers. With Stripe connected: read-only checks (account status, whether payouts are enabled). No
charges, refunds, or payouts.

**S5 Shipping.** Weigh and measure each product packed, compare carrier rates through the platform's calculator
(cited), and pick flat or calculated shipping, a free-shipping threshold (optional), packaging list, and processing
time. Add an "order to shipped" SOP (operations-setup).

**S6 Policies** (drafts, with the disclaimer "Not legal advice. Confirm with a licensed attorney."):
- Returns and refunds: window, condition, who pays return shipping, exceptions (custom or personalized items,
  consumables), damaged-item process, how to start a return.
- Privacy policy: what's collected, why, processors (platform, payments, email tool), cookies, rights and contact.
  Flag state privacy laws and GDPR if they sell to the EU.
- Terms of service: orders, pricing errors, shipping and risk of loss, IP, limitation of liability, governing law.
- Shipping-time promises must match reality. FTC Mail Order Rule:
  https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule

**S7 Test order.** Place a real or test-mode order end to end (checkout, email receipt, fulfillment, refund in test
mode). Note anything broken on the board.
