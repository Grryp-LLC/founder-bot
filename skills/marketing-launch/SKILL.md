# Marketing launch (MARKETING stage)

**M1 Positioning worksheet** (fill it together, 1 line each): customer (who, where they hang out), problem or desire,
product, the one reason it's different, proof (materials, process, reviews), price position, brand voice (3
adjectives), tagline options (3). If they need a name, offer 10 options with a one-line rationale each, then run M2 and
the trademark knockout (incorporate, L10) on the top 3.

**M2 Domain and handles.** Check domain availability through a registrar's public search or WHOIS/RDAP, and handles by
visiting each platform's public profile URL (Instagram, TikTok, X, Pinterest, YouTube, Facebook, Etsy shop name).
Report a table: available / taken / unclear. Never buy or register anything. If they want one, give the exact steps
and let them buy it.

**M3 Email list.** Pick a tool that fits their platform (for example, the platform's built-in email, or a dedicated
tool). Look up free-tier limits live. Checklist: signup form on the store, a welcome email (draft it), a simple
first-order incentive (optional), double opt-in if the tool supports it, physical mailing address in the footer and an
unsubscribe link. CAN-SPAM guide: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

**M4 First 30 days of social posts.** Deliver a table (day, platform, format, hook, caption draft, CTA, asset needed),
built on 4 pillars: behind the scenes/process, product close-ups, customer or use-case stories, and founder journey.
Mix in launch countdown posts (the Launch Board card is built for this). 3–5 posts a week is plenty for one person.
Drafts only. The owner posts. Endorsements and giveaways follow FTC rules:
https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing

**M5 Launch-day plan.** A T-minus timeline: T-14 teaser and waitlist, T-7 product reveals, T-3 email to list, T-0
launch post + email + friends-and-family ask for honest reviews, T+3 thank-you and a first-week recap, T+7 retro on the
weekly review. Put dates on the board. Calendar events only with a yes.

**M6 Small ad test plan.** Only after organic posts show which product and message resonate. The plan: one platform,
one product, 2–3 creatives, one audience, a fixed total budget and stop date **the owner sets**, success metric (cost per
add-to-cart or purchase), and a kill rule. You never create, launch, or fund ads.
