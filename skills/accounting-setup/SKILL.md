# Accounting setup (MONEY stage)

Tax items end with: "Not tax advice. Confirm with a CPA or enrolled agent."

**A1 Bookkeeping tool.** Options: a spreadsheet (fine for the first months at low volume), or a bookkeeping app that
connects to their bank and sales platform. Compare 2–3 on price, bank and platform integrations, invoicing, and sales
tax handling. Look up current prices live and cite them. Recommend one based on volume and budget.

**A2 Starter chart of accounts** (adapt names to the tool):
- Income: Product sales, Shipping income, Refunds and discounts (contra), Other income
- Cost of goods sold: Materials, Packaging, Inbound shipping, Print-on-demand or production costs
- Expenses: Platform and payment fees, Outbound shipping and postage, Advertising, Software and subscriptions,
  Equipment (ask a CPA about expensing vs. depreciating), Office and supplies, Professional fees, Licenses and filing
  fees, Bank fees, Education, Travel, Meals (limits apply)
- Assets: Business checking, Inventory, Equipment. Liabilities: Business credit card, Sales tax payable. Equity:
  Owner contributions, Owner draws.

**A3 Separate the money.** Business account and card used only for business, owner contributions and draws recorded
as such, and no personal spending on business accounts. That separation matters for LLC liability protection too.

**A4 Receipts workflow.** Snap or forward every receipt the same day into one place (bookkeeping app inbox, or a Drive
folder "Receipts/YYYY-MM"). Name files `YYYY-MM-DD_vendor_category`. Match receipts during the monthly close. If they
want a Gmail filter or label, give them the steps. Don't change their mailbox without a yes. Records guidance:
https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping

**A5 Estimated tax calendar.** Individuals with self-employment income generally pay quarterly estimated taxes.
Pull the four due dates **for the current tax year** from the IRS Form 1040-ES page
(https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1040-es) or Publication 505, and cite them. Weekend or holiday dates roll to
the next business day (https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes). Also look up
their state's estimated tax, franchise or annual report tax, and sales tax filing dates. Add each as a board task with a
due date. With a yes, add them to Calendar as all-day events with a 7-day reminder.

**A6 Monthly close checklist** (first week of each month): categorize all transactions, match receipts, reconcile bank
and card to statements, record platform payouts and fees, count inventory (or spot-check), set aside the tax
percentage their CPA suggests into a tax savings account, review profit and loss vs. last month, note sales tax due, and
add a one-line note to the weekly review.
