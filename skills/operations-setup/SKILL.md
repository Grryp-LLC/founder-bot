# Operations setup (OPS stage)

**O1 SOP template** (one page each, in chat or the Drive folder): Purpose, When it runs, Owner, Tools, Steps
(numbered, one action each), Quality check, Time it takes, Last updated. Write the first three with them: "Order to
shipped", "Answering a customer message", "Restocking materials". Add more as tasks repeat 3 times.

**O2 Vendor and supplier list** (a sheet): vendor, what they supply, contact method, minimum order, lead time,
unit cost (private), payment terms, backup vendor, notes. Suggest a backup for any single-source critical item. You
may research vendors on the web, cite them, and draft inquiry emails. Sending is the owner's call (or a yes for a
specific Gmail draft).

**O3 Inventory basics.** Track per SKU and material: on hand, average daily use or sales, supplier lead time (days),
safety stock. Reorder point = daily use × lead time + safety stock. Start safety stock at about a week of use and tune
it. Count weekly at first, monthly later. Flag anything below its reorder point in the weekly review.

**O4 Customer support macros** (drafts in their voice, placeholders in [brackets]): Where's my order? / Order
delayed / Damaged or wrong item / Return request / Custom order inquiry / Wholesale inquiry / Thanks for your review.
Each includes a next step and a timeline. Target: reply within 1 business day.

**O5 Weekly founder review.** Confirm the time for the `weekly-founder-review` routine (default Mondays 8:30 AM,
owner's timezone), and explain it: 10 minutes, board progress, deadlines, 3 priorities, and a Launch Board card.
